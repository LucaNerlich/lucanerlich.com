This site now has a proper blog powered by Docusaurus, sitting alongside the existing documentation.

<!-- truncate -->

The docs stay the long-form reference material for AEM, Java, JavaScript, Strapi, and related topics.
The blog is for shorter notes, announcements, and updates that do not belong in a guide.

Posts live in the `blog/` directory, use the classic Docusaurus blog plugin, and show up at `/blog/`.
RSS, Atom, and JSON feeds are available under that path as well.
