## Most-used AEM guides

These are the reference pages that keep getting the most traffic:

- [Touch UI Component Dialogs](/aem/component-dialogs/) -- 2,800+ lines of copy-paste-ready Granite UI dialog XML:
  empty templates, complex fields, show/hide logic, multifields, RTE config, and validation.
- [Groovy Console Scripts](/aem/groovy-console/) -- 30+ ready-to-run script templates for bulk activation, content
  migration, workflow ops, and day-to-day AEM maintenance.
- [Dialog Validation](/aem/components/dialog-validation/) -- built-in Granite UI validation, custom validators, and
  practical patterns that stop bad content before it hits the JCR.

Browse the rest of the [AEM section](/aem/) when you need architecture, Sling Models, HTL, clientlibs, or recipes.

## Omarchy plugins

I also ship four [Omarchy](https://omarchy.org/) Quattro bar widgets (Rust backend + QML frontend for Hyprland).
Docs and install notes live under [Projects](/projects/):

- [Coolify QS](/projects/coolify-qs/) -- live Coolify deployment activity in the bar, per-server panels, and desktop
  notifications when a deploy finishes or fails.
- [WireView Pro 2 QS](/projects/wireview-pro2-qs/) -- live GPU power draw from the Thermal Grizzly WireView Pro II,
  with a details panel and optional per-pin sensor data.
- [Omastonk](/projects/omastonk/) -- a rotating market watchlist with live quotes in the bar and per-symbol charts in
  the panel.
- [Obsidian Daily QS](/projects/obsidian-daily-qs/) -- today's Obsidian daily-note todos in the bar, with a panel to
  check, add, search, and carry items over.

Marketplace listings: [plugins by Luca Nerlich](https://omarchyplugins.com/?author=LucaNerlich).
